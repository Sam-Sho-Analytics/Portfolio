About My Portfolio

Welcome to my Business Intelligence and Data Analytics portfolio. I focus on using data to help businesses understand performance, identify operational challenges, uncover growth opportunities and make informed decisions. This portfolio showcases practical analytics projects across E-commerce, Financial Services, Customer Behaviour, Sales Performance and Operations, demonstrating how business data can be transformed into insights that address real-world business questions. Through these projects, I apply data preparation, analysis, data modelling and visualisation to examine key business performance indicators, identify trends and patterns, and develop evidence-based findings and recommendations. My approach goes beyond presenting dashboards and numbers. It is about understanding what the data reveals about business performance, why it matters and what actions businesses can consider to improve results.
