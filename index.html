About My Portfolio

## About My Portfolio Welcome to my Business Intelligence and Data Analytics portfolio. This portfolio showcases my practical application of data analytics to business problems, demonstrating how raw data can be transformed into meaningful insights that support informed decision-making and improved business performance. My projects explore different dimensions of business performance, including **E-commerce Sales and Revenue Analytics, Bank Customer Churn Analysis, Customer Behaviour and Retention, Financial Performance, Marketing Effectiveness and Inventory Management.** Using tools such as **Microsoft Power BI, SQL, Microsoft Excel and Python**, I explore the analytics process, from data preparation and transformation to data modelling, visualisation, KPI development and the communication of actionable business insights. Each project presents the business problem, analytical approach, key performance indicators (KPIs), findings and recommendations, supported by interactive dashboards, datasets and project documentation where applicable. My objective is to demonstrate how data analytics can help organisations understand performance, identify underlying business challenges, uncover opportunities and make better strategic and operational decisions.
